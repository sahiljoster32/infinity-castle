Enter the Infinity CastleDemon Slayer · Kimetsu no Yaiba fan experience

Ahead of you, past three paper seals on the Pilgrim's Way, burns the Heart of the castle. Each seal burns away when you find one of the hidden biwa players, who sit in quiet courtyards, one to a district. Listen for them: every time a biwa sounds, the rooms around you shift, and the sound comes from where the player sits.

Open sliding doors, find paper charms with lore and key charms that open locked doors and sealed gates, and look for the rare rooms: a flooded hall, a gilded throne room, a library of scrolls and an upside-down tea house. Your map keeps a record of where you've walked, until the biwa rewrites it.

An unofficial fan-made interactive experience inspired by Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba and its Infinity Castle. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by the rights holders.
